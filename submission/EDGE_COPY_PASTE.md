## 3. Detailed Description
Refract your prompts into clarity.

Prism is an AI prompt coach that lives right next to your chat input on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Perplexity. Type (or select) a prompt, hit the 💎 button or Ctrl+Shift+E, and Prism shows you exactly what's weak about it — then hands you a stronger version.

WHAT YOU GET
• Smart analysis — clarity, specificity, context and constraint scores (0–100) computed locally, instantly.
• One-click optimization — side-by-side original vs optimized. Edit it, copy it, or replace your prompt in place.
• Template library — 10 professional templates (code review, emails, study plans, brainstorming and more) plus your own, with {placeholder} support. Templates insert directly into the chat box — no popup dialogs.
• History & favorites — every session saved locally; star the best prompts and reuse them.
• Adaptive learning — Prism notices what kinds of tasks you run often and tunes its suggestions.
• Works with your AI account — bring your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or run fully local with Ollama. No account? The built-in rule engine works offline, forever, free.

PRIVACY FIRST
Your prompts are analyzed 100% on your device. Nothing is collected, tracked or transmitted by default. Cloud AI is strictly opt-in: only the current prompt text is sent, only to the provider you choose, with your own encrypted key (AES-GCM). No analytics. No telemetry. No remote code.

Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+E (⌘+Shift+E on macOS).

## 9. Permissions Justification
- storage: Save user settings, history, favorites, and encrypted API keys locally.
- activeTab: Access the active tab to inject the Prism coaching UI.
- scripting: Inject the coach button and analysis panel into AI chat pages.
- clipboardWrite: Copy the optimized prompt to the clipboard.
- contextMenus: Provide a right-click "Coach this prompt" shortcut.
- host_permissions: Required to interact with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, and AI provider APIs.
